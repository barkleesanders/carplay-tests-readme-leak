# People named in the document

The measurement reference names two Apple employees. This page records
only what the document attributes to them and what public *professional*
sources independently show. No personal contact details, addresses, or
family information were collected or are published here.

## Kelly Cole — named on the TC-2a stats-buffer problem

**In the document:** quoted directly ("Per Kelly Cole on rdar://160878215")
explaining, with a timestamped trace, why the `DPCreated` event rolls off
the accessory's ~10 s stats buffer before reaching the iPhone; credited
with filing `rdar://182233742` ("Cache stats events for iOS consumption on
incoming iOS Statistics Request") to track the fix.

**Public footprint:** effectively none that can be tied to Apple. Targeted
searches across professional-profile aggregators, Google Patents (Apple as
assignee), GitHub, conference/WWDC records, and the radar numbers
themselves returned no source connecting any Kelly Cole to Apple, CarPlay,
automotive, or iOS. Radar is Apple's closed internal system, so the
attributions can be neither corroborated nor refuted publicly.

- One unverified lead, deliberately **not** merged: a 2016 University of
  Illinois news item names a "Kelly Cole, a senior in computer engineering"
  as developer of a campus food app. No Apple connection is stated; it may
  be a different person.
- Numerous public Kelly Coles in unrelated fields (telecom policy, aviation
  sales, academia, film, publishing) were checked and ruled out.
